KAVORENZA · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Kavorenza processes only the information needed to run shared multiplayer sessions.

1. Controller

appsmakerdeluxe Studios
appsmakerdeluxe@gmail.com

2. Data we process

  • Anonymous Firebase user ID: to identify a player inside a room and enforce security rules.
  • Optional nickname: to show the player to others in the room.
  • Room and game data: room code, player seat, game selection, moves and game state for real-time synchronisation.

3. What Kavorenza does not use

No GPS location, contacts, photos, videos, microphone or calendar. No required email address or telephone number. The MVP has no advertising, AdMob, Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics.

4. Firebase and deletion

Kavorenza uses Firebase for anonymous sign-in and Cloud Firestore for game synchronisation. An EU data region will be selected. Anonymous accounts are automatically cleaned up after 30 days; room and game data are intended for short sessions and should be deleted within 24 hours.

5. Your rights

Where applicable law provides, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability and object to processing. You may also complain to a data protection authority.
