KAVORENZA · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Kavorenza processes only the data needed to run shared multiplayer sessions.

1. Controller

AppsMakerDeluxe Studios
appsmakerdeluxe@gmail.com

2. Data we process

  • Anonymous Firebase user ID: room membership and security rules.
  • Optional nickname: shown to other players if you enter one.
  • Room and game data: room code, player seat, game selection, connection status, moves and game state for real-time synchronisation.

3. What Kavorenza does not collect

No GPS location, contacts, photos, videos, microphone or calendar. No email address or phone number is required. The MVP uses no advertising, AdMob, Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics.

4. Firebase and retention

Firebase is used for anonymous sign-in and Cloud Firestore for game synchronisation. Room data and related player, nickname, vote and move documents receive an expiry time 24 hours after room creation. Firebase processes automatic deletion asynchronously; expired documents may remain stored for up to a further 24 hours. Subcollections have separate expiry timestamps because deleting a room document does not automatically delete its subcollections. Anonymous Firebase accounts are automatically cleaned up after 30 days.

5. Your rights

Where applicable law provides, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability and object to processing. You may also complain to a data protection authority. Contact us at the email address above.
