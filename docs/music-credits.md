# Kavorenza music credits

The browser player randomly selects one of 15 in-game tracks. Audio can be switched off at any time.

- 12 tracks: CoMiGo's Short Music Loops, Cosmo Myzrail Gorynych — CC0 1.0: https://comigo.itch.io/music-loops
- 3 tracks: 8 Bit Retro RPG Music Asset Pack, dancramp — CC0 1.0: https://dancramp.itch.io/8-bit-retro-rpg-music-asset-pack

The browser-hosted copies are bundled in `firebase-public/audio/`; Android copies are bundled in `app/src/main/res/raw/` in the private Kavorenza app repository. No third-party audio service is contacted at playback time.

Credits are voluntary; both artists label their respective packs CC0. The source pages describe unrestricted use/commercial use: [CoMiGo Short Music Loops](https://comigo.itch.io/music-loops), [dancramp 8 Bit Retro RPG Music](https://dancramp.itch.io/8-bit-retro-rpg-music-asset-pack).
